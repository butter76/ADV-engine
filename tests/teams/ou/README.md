# ADV OU sample teams

`sample-teams.txt` is 50 teams in Showdown's export text, from the pokepast.es
links of Smogon's "ADV OU Sample Teams" thread
(https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/3687813/), as the hoenn project
(https://github.com/pokemon-labs/hoenn, `teams/ou/`) gathered them and edited
them to pass Showdown's gen3ou validator.

They are what `tests/export_test.cpp` reads, and teams for anything that needs
a few real ones.
